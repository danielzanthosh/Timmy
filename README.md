Have you ever thought of managing your study time better than you already have?
Well, I guess you've used something like a Pomodoro timer for that, right? Okay, you take the timer app on your, put it on your desk, and boom a notification. And here's Timmy, a distraction free Desktop Utility with a screen, a nobe, and a gyroscope.


## Authors

- [Daniel Santhosh](https://www.github.com/danielzanthosh)


## Features

- A Web Dashbord for settings
- A Gyroscope for quick actions
- A Rotary Encoder for quick timer setting change.
- A 0.96 OLED for viewing
- And a Buzzer to wake you up
